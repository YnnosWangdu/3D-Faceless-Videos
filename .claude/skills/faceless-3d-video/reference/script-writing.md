# Writing scripts that hold attention

## Structure of a 30–60 s short (90–150 words, 7–12 beats)

1. **Hook (beat 1, under 2.5 s):** a surprising claim, a number or a question. No intros, no
   "hey guys", no "in this video".
   - "If you dropped Everest into the ocean, it would disappear."
   - "This metal is worth more than gold, and you're holding some right now."
2. **Context (1–2 beats):** why it matters, or the stakes.
3. **Payoff beats (4–7):** one fact, step or reason per beat. Escalate, and save the best for last.
4. **Twist or kicker:** the most surprising detail.
5. **Loop or CTA (last beat, 2–3 s):** either a short CTA ("Follow for more…") or a final line that
   flows back into the hook, so a replay feels seamless.

Long-form 16:9 videos (2–6 min) use the same rhythm in chapters. Every 30–45 s, open a new
"curiosity loop" ("but that's not the strangest part…").

## Sentence rules for TTS

- Write for the ear. Keep sentences under 20 words and use contractions.
- Spell numbers the way they should be said if TTS might misread them: "eleven thousand metres",
  "nineteen sixty". On-screen numbers in `scene` stay as digits.
- Avoid parentheses, slashes, emojis and abbreviations the voice can't say ("approx.", "vs.").
- One idea per beat. If a beat's narration runs over about 25 words (~9 s), split it.
- Use punctuation for pacing: commas give short pauses, full stops give longer ones.

## Niches that work well with this kit

| niche | templates that carry it | style |
|---|---|---|
| Science / space facts | globe, counter, bar_chart, abstract | space, neon |
| Finance / economy | counter, bar_chart, versus, city | noir, clay |
| History | timeline, landscape, quote, image_card | sunset, noir |
| Tech / AI explainers | list, abstract, city, hero | neon, space |
| Nature / ocean | globe, landscape, counter | ocean, forest |
| Psychology / self-improvement | title, list, quote, abstract | pastel, clay |
| Top-N / rankings | list, versus, bar_chart | any |
| Products / "how it works" | hero, versus, list | clay, noir |

## Accuracy and platform hygiene

- Check facts before they go in the script. Prefer round, defensible numbers. If the user supplies
  claims, keep their wording but flag anything doubtful.
- AI voice plus synthetic visuals: YouTube and TikTok require you to disclose realistic altered or
  synthetic content. Abstract 3D graphics usually don't count, but remind the user of the setting.
- Only use music and images the user has rights to. For music, suggest the YouTube Audio Library or
  royalty-free tracks.
